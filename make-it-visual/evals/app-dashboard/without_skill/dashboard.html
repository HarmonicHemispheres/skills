    Tips for a smoother service

    Small habits that get the most out of Pulse

    1. Keep the Ready column short

      Food that waits more than 5 minutes gets cold. When an order turns red, tap Remind to text the customer.

    2. Watch prep time during the rush

      If the last-30-minutes marker passes the 15-minute goal, switch on Busy mode so pickup times stay honest.

    3. Compare Fridays with Fridays

      The arrows compare you with the same day last week, not yesterday. A Tuesday is never as busy as a Friday.

    4. Prep from your top sellers

      Shawarma bowls are selling about 60 a day this week. Marinate enough chicken the night before.

    5. Not sure what a number means?

      Tap the next to any heading for a plain-English explanation.
